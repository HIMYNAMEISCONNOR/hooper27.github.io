HOW TO PLAY

WASD / ARROWS — move
SHIFT — turbo (drains stamina)
SPACE (hold + release) — shoot; release in the green window for a guaranteed make
SPACE (run at the rim) — dunk or layup
E — pass (with ball) / steal (on defense)
Q — crossover, break ankles
SPACE (on defense) — jump to contest and block shots
P / ESC — pause
METER — shot value fades with distance; defenders lower your odds. Contested green windows shrink.
ON FIRE — hit 3 in a row and your next shots get a huge boost until you miss.
BADGES — every legend plays to their real style: Shaq posters, Curry rains deep, Kyrie shakes ankles, PG locks up.
STAMINA — the little bars under players. Dead legs = weak shots and slow dribbles.
